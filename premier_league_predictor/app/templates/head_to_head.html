⚔️ Competition format

Every player faces every other player twice: once at home and once away. Home and away determine the fixture order only and do not provide a scoring advantage.

Each fixture compares the players' complete Premier League prediction score for that gameweek, including Double Points.

📊 League tie-breaks

  1. Cockfight Cup league points
  2. Head-to-head points
  3. Head-to-head gameweek score difference
  4. Total gameweek points across the competition
  5. Current Premier League position

🏆 The final

The top two players after the league stage meet in Gameweek 38. The higher Gameweek 38 prediction score wins the MCFG Cockfight Cup and the £20 prize.

If the final scores are level, the player who finished higher in the Cockfight Cup league wins.
