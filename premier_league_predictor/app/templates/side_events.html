⚽ Competition format

The Champions League competition uses the same prediction-league format as the Premier League competition. Players predict the score of every eligible match and build a cumulative points total.

It is a points league, not an elimination competition: every player's scores continue to count through to the Champions League final.

📅 Eligible fixtures

Predictions begin with the Champions League knockout stage and include every eligible fixture through to the final.

Each prediction locks at that match's kick-off. Other players' predictions remain hidden until the fixture starts.

Each player can select one Double Points fixture in every Champions League prediction round. Double Points doubles the complete award, including the exact-score bonus.

📊 League order

Players are ranked by their total Champions League prediction points. If totals are level, the order is:

  1. Correct draws
  2. Correct scores
  3. Correct winners
  4. Player name alphabetically

🏆 Competition winner

After the Champions League final is settled, the player at the top of the prediction table wins the competition and the £20 prize.
