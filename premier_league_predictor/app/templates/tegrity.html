What does Tegridy do?

Whenever someone saves or changes a prediction, Preddies adds a new entry to a protected timeline. Each entry is linked to the one before it, rather like numbered pages in a sealed record book.

If somebody changes a prediction directly in the database, removes an entry or rewrites the history, those links will no longer match and Tegridy will raise a warning.

What can players see?

While everything is healthy, Tegridy shows only that the checks passed and how many changes were checked. It does not reveal anyone's prediction history.

If a problem is detected, the relevant audit details become visible so the group can see what needs investigating. Predictions for matches that have not kicked off remain hidden.

What does a green check mean?

It means the saved predictions agree with the protected timeline and the chain has not been broken. It gives the whole league a shared, independent warning system rather than asking players to rely on an administrator's word alone.
