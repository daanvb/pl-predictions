⏱️ Automatic refresh sequence

Times are UK time. This is the order the background worker keeps data current.

1. Around live kick-off
Every 60 seconds while a tracked match is live or in its live window. Scores, status, clocks, scorers and cards are checked.
2. Two hours after a Premier League gameweek ends
Premier League fixtures and match-stat/head-to-head history refresh. The next gameweek opens at 09:00 UK time the following day.
3. Sunday from 20:00
Champions League fixtures and head-to-head history refresh for the coming match block. The CL opening Signal message sends only after both updates finish.
4. Quiet periods
The worker checks again at least every 6 hours; automatic database backups also run every 6 hours.
